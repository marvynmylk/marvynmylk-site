Results come from the Alabama Secretary of State's county precinct files for the November 3, 2026 general election, added up across counties. Totals include write-ins. Results are unofficial until certified. The files for Blount, Conecuh, Cullman, Geneva, Henry, Macon, Marengo, Marion and Sumter counties weren't available yet, so districts that include those counties show partial totals, and three districts aren't listed.

District lines come from the U.S. Census Bureau's 2024 boundary files. A federal court redrew some State Senate districts in the Montgomery area in 2025, so the Senate map there may not match the 2026 lines exactly.
