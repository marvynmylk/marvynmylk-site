Questions about Hampton Cove

How did Hampton Cove vote in 2026?

The 3 precincts that vote in Hampton Cove went for Republican Tommy Tuberville for governor, 5,635 votes to 3,825. In 2024, the precincts that existed then voted for Donald Trump, 4,636 to 1,944.

Did Hampton Cove flip?

Hampton Cove moved 21 points toward Democrats, from R+40.9 to R+19.8.

Who represents Hampton Cove in the Alabama Legislature?

Depending on your address, Hampton Cove voters are in State Senate, District 7, State House, District 20, State House, District 22. Each card on this page shows the 2026 result districtwide and in Hampton Cove.

How many people in Hampton Cove didn't vote?

About 9,327 registered voters in Hampton Cove's precincts didn't cast a ballot in 2026.

Where do I vote in Hampton Cove?

Hampton Cove voters are assigned to one of 3 polling places, depending on their address. Check your polling place on the Alabama Secretary of State's site before every election, since assignments can change.
