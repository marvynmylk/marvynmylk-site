Questions about Meridianville

How did Meridianville vote in 2026?

The two precincts that vote in Meridianville went for Democrat Doug Jones for governor, 3,454 votes to 2,428 for Tommy Tuberville. In 2024, the same precincts voted for Donald Trump, 3,794 to 2,816.

Did Meridianville flip?

Yes. Both Meridianville precincts, at Meridianville Church of Christ and First Baptist Church Meridianville, voted Republican for president in 2024 and Democratic for governor in 2026.

What state House and Senate districts is Meridianville in?

The Meridianville Church of Christ precinct votes in Alabama Senate District 7 and House District 21. In 2026, Democrat Jared Sluss won Senate District 7 by 646 votes, and Republican Rex Reynolds held House District 21 by 654 votes.

How many people in Meridianville didn't vote?

About 6,758 registered voters in the two Meridianville precincts didn't cast a ballot in 2026. That's more than 6 times Jones's margin there.

Where do I vote in Meridianville?

Meridianville voters are assigned to either Meridianville Church of Christ or First Baptist Church Meridianville, depending on their address. Check your polling place on the Alabama Secretary of State's site before every election, since assignments can change.
