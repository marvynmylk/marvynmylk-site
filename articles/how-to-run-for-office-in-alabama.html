How to run for office in Alabama

In 2026, 85 of Alabama's 137 legislative races had only one name on the ballot. In those districts, the election was over before anyone voted. Here's why that matters, and exactly how to run in 2030.

Short answer

To run for the Alabama House you must be at least 21; for the Senate, 25. You need to have lived in Alabama for 3 years and in your district for 1 year before the election. Party candidates qualify with their party in January of the election year. Independents qualify by petition.

Why an empty ballot line matters

When nobody runs, there's no debate, no one asking hard questions and no reason for the winner to listen. The voters in that district don't get a say.

It also costs votes higher up the ballot. A local candidate knocks on doors, puts out yard signs and gives neighbors a reason to show up. Those voters then vote in every race on the ballot. The same is true for Republicans, which is why both parties try to fill every seat.

Questions people ask

How old do you have to be to run for the Alabama Legislature?

You must be at least 21 to run for the Alabama House of Representatives and at least 25 to run for the Alabama Senate.

What are the residency rules to run for the Alabama Legislature?

You must have lived in Alabama for at least three years and in your district for at least one year before the election.

When is the next Alabama legislative election?

All 105 House seats and 35 Senate seats are on the ballot every four years. After 2026, the next regular legislative election is in 2030, with party qualifying in January of that year.

How many Alabama legislative races were uncontested in 2026?

In the Secretary of State's 2026 general election results, 85 of 137 legislative races had only one candidate: 59 Republicans and 26 Democrats.

Can I run as an independent in Alabama?

Yes. Independent candidates skip the party primary and qualify by petition, with signatures from registered voters equal to 3% of the vote for governor in the district in the last general election. You can't run as an independent if you ran in that year's primary.
