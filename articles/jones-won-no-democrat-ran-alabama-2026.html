Questions people ask

Did Doug Jones win any Alabama districts with no Democrat on the ballot?

Yes. Jones beat Tommy Tuberville in six Alabama House districts where Republicans ran unopposed for the Legislature in 2026: Districts 3, 44, 46, 73, 79 and 104, based on polling-place results.

How many Alabama legislative races were unopposed in 2026?

85 of 137 Alabama legislative races had only one candidate in 2026: 59 Republicans and 26 Democrats.

Which Alabama legislative seats flipped in 2026?

Democrats Jared Sluss (Senate District 7), Allison Montgomery (House District 25) and Nell Brown (House District 15) won seats held by Republicans.

When are Alabama legislative seats up again?

Alabama elects its whole Legislature every four years. The next regular election is in 2030.

Results come from the Alabama Secretary of State's 2026 precinct files and are unofficial until certified.

Governor results inside each district are added up from polling places. Where a polling place serves more than one district, its votes are split by how many ballots were cast in each district's race. Absentee ballots are counted countywide and aren't included, so these are close estimates, not official district totals. Legislative race results are official districtwide totals. Precinct files for Blount, Conecuh, Cullman, Geneva, Henry, Macon, Marengo, Marion and Sumter counties weren't available, so districts reaching into those counties are based on their other counties.
