Alabama almost elected a Democratic governor

In 2020, Tommy Tuberville beat Doug Jones for U.S. Senate by 471,598 votes. In 2026, the same two men ran for governor, and Tuberville won by 66,308. Every county with posted results moved toward Jones. Here's where it happened, and what it would have taken.

The suburbs are the surprise. Even with turnout down from a presidential year, Jones held on to 96% of his voters there. In Shelby, Limestone and St. Clair, he got more votes than he did in 2020. Tuberville kept only about 67%.

The Black Belt is where the most room is left. Jones won those counties, but only about 79% of his 2020 voters came back, the lowest of any group.

What it would have taken

Tuberville won by 66,308 votes. At least 926,479 registered Alabamians didn't vote.

That's about 7 out of every 100 people who stayed home, and that count only covers the 58 counties with files posted.

Here's another way to see it. If Jones's 2020 voters in the rest of the state had come back at the same rate as in the suburbs, he'd have picked up about 75,600 more votes. That's more than the margin, before counting a single new voter.

Questions people ask

Who won the 2026 Alabama governor's race?

Republican Tommy Tuberville beat Democrat Doug Jones 872,561 votes to 806,253 in unofficial results, about 52% to 48%. It was the closest Alabama governor's race since 2002.

How close was Tuberville vs. Jones?

Tuberville won by 66,308 votes, about 4 points. In 2020, when the same two men ran for U.S. Senate, Tuberville won by 471,598 votes, about 20 points.

Which Alabama counties flipped to Doug Jones in 2026?

Six counties Tuberville won in 2020 voted for Jones in 2026: Tuscaloosa, Madison, Mobile, Clarke, Barbour and Conecuh. Jones carried 19 counties in all, the most for a Democratic candidate for governor since 2010.

Did every Alabama county move toward Democrats?

Of the 58 counties with 2026 precinct files posted so far, all 58 moved toward Jones compared with the 2020 Senate race. The biggest moves were in Shelby, Limestone, St. Clair and Jefferson counties.

Why did Tuberville get so many fewer votes than in 2020?

2020 was a presidential year, so turnout was always going to fall. But it fell unevenly. Tuberville got 519,515 fewer votes than in 2020. Jones got only 114,225 fewer. Jones held on to about 88% of his 2020 total; Tuberville held on to about 63%.

How many Alabama voters stayed home in 2026?

In the 58 counties with precinct files, at least 926,479 registered voters didn't cast a ballot. Tuberville's statewide margin was 66,308, about 7% of that number.

Is Alabama becoming a swing state?

Not yet. Republicans still won Alabama's other statewide races by 8 points or more in 2026. But a 4-point governor's race, with every county moving the same direction, shows Alabama is much closer than its usual results suggest, and that turnout decides how close.
