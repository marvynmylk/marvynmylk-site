Alabama's 5th District was closer than you think.

In 2024, nobody ran against Rep. Dale Strong. In 2026, Democrat Andrew Sneed did, and came within 17,075 votes while winning Madison County outright.

Short answer

Republican Dale Strong won Alabama's 5th Congressional District in 2026, 142,386 votes to 125,311 for Democrat Andrew Sneed, a margin of 17,075 votes (6.4 points).

From no race to a real race

In 2024, Strong's name was the only one on the ballot. Two years later, a Democrat on the ballot got 125,311 votes. That's what happens when someone runs.

The district, county by county

North Alabama's 5th District stretches along the Tennessee line. Sneed won the biggest county, Madison, by 17,889 votes. Strong won the other five.

Questions people ask

Who won Alabama's 5th Congressional District in 2026?

Republican Rep. Dale Strong won, 142,386 votes to 125,311 for Democrat Andrew Sneed, according to unofficial county results.

How close was the Alabama 5th District race?

Strong won by 17,075 votes, about 6.4 percentage points.

What counties are in Alabama's 5th Congressional District?

The 5th District includes all or part of Madison, Limestone, Morgan, Jackson, Lawrence and Lauderdale counties.

Did a Democrat run in the 5th District in 2024?

No. Dale Strong was the only candidate on the 2024 general election ballot.

Did Andrew Sneed win Madison County?

Yes. Sneed won the Madison County portion of the district 89,062 to 71,173.
