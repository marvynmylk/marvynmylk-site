Every precinct in Madison County moved left

In 2024, Donald Trump carried Madison County by 17,606 votes. In 2026, Doug Jones carried it by 26,115. Of the 74 precincts I could compare, all 74 moved toward Democrats, and 26 flipped.

That's a 25-point swing in two years, and it wasn't just one race. In the 2026 U.S. Senate race, Democrat Everett Wess also carried Madison County, 87,854 votes to Barry Moore's 71,817.

Even the reddest places moved

About these numbers

All results come from the Alabama Secretary of State's precinct files. Precinct percentages count votes cast at the polling place and leave out absentee and provisional ballots, which are reported for the county as a whole. A few polling places were renamed between elections; I matched them by name and location, and five 2026 precincts with no clear 2024 match are listed without a change. The 2026 results are unofficial until certified.

These are two different races with different candidates, so this isn't a perfect apples-to-apples comparison. But the direction is the same in every precinct I could compare.

What this means

Madison County didn't change overnight. People ran for office, knocked doors, and showed up. Every one of those precinct numbers is a group of neighbors who decided their vote was worth casting.
