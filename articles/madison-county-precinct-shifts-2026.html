Every precinct in Madison County moved left

In 2024, Donald Trump carried Madison County by 17,606 votes. In 2026, Doug Jones carried it by 26,115. Of the 74 precincts I could compare, all 74 moved toward Democrats, and 26 flipped.

That's a 25-point swing in two years, and it wasn't just one race. In the 2026 U.S. Senate race, Democrat Everett Wess also carried Madison County, 87,854 votes to Barry Moore's 71,817.

Jones got more votes than Harris did. In a midterm, with about 36,000 fewer ballots cast, Doug Jones won 92,997 votes in Madison County. Kamala Harris got 87,824 in the 2024 presidential race.

Precinct percentages count votes cast at the polling place and leave out absentee and provisional ballots, which are reported for the county as a whole. A few polling places were renamed between elections; I matched them by name and location, and five 2026 precincts with no clear 2024 match are listed without a change.
