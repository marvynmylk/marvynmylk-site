House District 25 flipped blue.

Democrat Allison Montgomery beat Republican Rep. Phillip Rigsby by 1,556 votes in 2026, flipping a North Alabama seat Rigsby had held since 2022. The district covers parts of Madison, Monrovia and the area near Capshaw, in Madison and Limestone counties.

Short answer

Alabama House District 25 is represented by Allison Montgomery (D), who won it in November 2026, 12,505 votes to 10,949.

Where it was won

Montgomery carried 9 of the district's 15 precincts. Her widest margins came at Sherwood Baptist and Hope Church. Rigsby's best were Asbury and Little Indian Creek.

The absentee edge

Absentee ballots in the Madison County part of the district went to Montgomery 604 to 406. That's a 198-vote edge before a single polling place reported, about one-eighth of her final margin.

What's next

Alabama House members serve four-year terms, so District 25's next regular election is in 2030. In a district decided by 1,556 votes, every new registration counts.

Questions people ask

Who represents Alabama House District 25?

Democrat Allison Montgomery won House District 25 in November 2026, beating Republican Rep. Phillip Rigsby 12,505 votes to 10,949, according to unofficial results.

What area does House District 25 cover?

District 25 covers parts of northwest Madison County, including parts of Madison and Monrovia, and part of Limestone County near Capshaw.

How close was the House District 25 race?

Montgomery won by 1,556 votes, about 6.6 percentage points.

When is the next House District 25 election?

Alabama House members serve four-year terms. The next regular election for District 25 is in 2030.
