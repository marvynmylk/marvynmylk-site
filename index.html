Alabama voters

Check your voter registration

  • Moved, changed your name, or skipped a few elections? Your registration can change without you knowing.
  • Alabama doesn't let you register in the 14 days before an election, so you can't fix it at the polls.
  • It takes about 30 seconds. Elections are decided by a handful of votes. Make sure yours counts.
